How to Use

  1. Select platform size: choose a target platform from the "Size" panel on the right, or enter a custom size.
  2. Choose background image: pick an Unsplash image, Bing wallpaper, or upload your own from the "Gallery" panel.
  3. Edit text: enter your title and subtitle in the "Text" panel, adjust font size and family.
  4. Adjust style: modify text color, background overlay and position in the "Style" panel.
  5. Download: click the "Download" button at the top right to export a single image, or use "Batch Export" to generate multiple platform sizes at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool really free?
Yes, FFEE Cover Design is completely free. No registration or login is required. All features are available directly, and exported images have no watermark.
Which platform sizes are supported?
It supports Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, Podcast and other global platforms, as well as Xiaohongshu, WeChat, Bilibili, Douyin and Zhihu, with 30+ sizes in total, plus custom sizes.
Do exported images have a watermark?
No. Exported images are completely clean and contain no watermark or brand logo.
Can Bing wallpapers be used commercially?
No. Bing daily wallpapers are for personal use only. They cannot be used commercially or uploaded to other platforms. For commercial use, please use Unsplash library or upload your own images.
Can I batch export multiple platform sizes?
Yes. Click the "Batch Export" button at the top, select the platforms you need, and export all adapted images in one click.
Are images uploaded to a server?
No. All image processing is done locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server, ensuring complete privacy.
Does it support mobile devices?
Yes. The tool is mobile-friendly and can be used in a mobile browser, but a desktop device is recommended for the best experience.
Can I save design templates?
Templates are not supported yet, but you can save the current page via browser bookmarks, or capture a screenshot to save your design parameters.
What is the resolution of exported images?
The resolution depends on the platform size you choose. For example, Twitter covers are 1600×900, YouTube thumbnails are 1280×720, all exported in the recommended HD sizes.
Do I need to install any plugin?
No. FFEE Cover Design is a pure web tool that works directly in your browser without any plugin or software.
